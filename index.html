03 / THE REAL REASON

THE BEST CUE
ISN'T COLOUR ALONE.

Colour vision deficiency can make certain football kits, balls and training equipment harder to distinguish. Better light–dark contrast, visible shirt numbers, patterns, positions and clear spoken instructions can support players, coaches and match officials.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Who created the Colour Blindness Gaming Hub?

Cornelius Aurelius is the creator, designer and developer of this original gaming hub and its Signal Academy activity. Visit the official Cornelius Aurelius website for the wider portfolio.

What do the games teach?

They encourage decisions based on more than colour alone: numbers, visual patterns, positions, contrast, shapes, memory and clear coaching signals.

Are these medical or vision tests?

No. These are educational experiences, not clinical tests or simulations of how any particular person sees. People with different forms of colour vision deficiency have different experiences.
