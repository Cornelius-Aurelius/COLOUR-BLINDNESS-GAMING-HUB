Why this hub exists

Colour-blind players should not have to guess in fast moments. These games are built to make alternative cues feel natural, quick and repeatable.

Simple missions One task per game so players understand what to do without a long tutorial.
Football thinking Train the kind of quick recognition that matters during passing, pressure and movement.
Colour is not enough Players practise using numbers, kit details, positions, signals and memory cues.
